<objective>
Extract real player season stats (passing/rushing/receiving/defense) from the Madden franchise file's PlayerStats table during sync, write them into PlayerSeason.stats as canonical lower_underscore keys, and avoid duplicate PlayerSeason rows on re-sync via [playerId+year] compound-index upsert.

Purpose: After a Madden sync, the Records leaderboard and PlayerProfilePage show real stat lines, not just an OVR rating. This unlocks downstream features (record book, dashboard widgets, narrative engine) that depend on populated PlayerSeason.stats.

Output: Updated `madden-reader.cjs` returning `playerStats: RawPlayerStat[]`; updated `madden-sync-service.ts` with `RawPlayerStat` interface, extended `ExtractResult` and `SyncDiff`, a `mapRawStatsToRecord` helper, and an upsert-aware `commitSyncDiff` that calls `updatePlayerSeason` when an existing PlayerSeason for [playerId+year] is found.
</objective>
